Support

Night clerk help for Lantern Ledger on iOS.

Restore purchases

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Apple confirms any active VIP subscription on this Apple ID. Coin packs are consumable and are not restored.

The desk

  • Tap the lantern to spend one energy and drop a piece.
  • Drag two matching pieces together to merge.
  • Deliver the current request, then read the case note.
