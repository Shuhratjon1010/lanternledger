Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. For the iOS game Lantern Ledger (com.lanternledger.game).

What we collect

Lantern Ledger does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no crash or usage telemetry sent to the developer.

Progress lives in a save file on your device. Sound preference is stored with that save. Nothing from the blotter is uploaded.

Purchases

Coin packs and the VIP subscription are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details, receipts, and subscription status. We do not receive your card number, Apple ID password, or billing address.
